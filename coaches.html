Fig. 00 — Coaches

Two brothers.
Eight years.
One method.

We started training together at 16. We’ve been arguing about training, nutrition, and recovery ever since. DND Coaching is what came out of those arguments — a method neither of us would have built alone.

You won’t find us running a 1,000-client roster off a template. Every client gets named, tracked, and adjusted weekly. We know your training pattern and your weekend habits. That’s the only way this works.

Coach Nº 01

Denis

Coach · Training + nutrition

Ever since I was little I wanted to get into sports but nothing ever resonated with me. I was always an athletic guy but never found something to stick to. That all changed when I turned 16 and joined the gym on a whim, just to see what it would be like.

I was hooked straight away — the feeling of progressing, getting stronger, getting better and levelling up. Ever since then I’ve been in love with the discipline, the consistency, the numbers going up, the body changing and adapting, the person I was becoming in the mirror. Eight years later you literally can’t keep me away from training even if you sent an army.

I came at coaching from the training side — programming, periodisation, exercise selection, the why-this-set-doesn’t-build-the-body-you-want side. But in DND we both work both halves. Every plan goes past both of us. I work best with people who’ve been training for a while and are tired of doing the same thing without results. If that sounds like you, we should probably talk.

Coach Nº 02

Darius

Coach · Training + nutrition

I came at this from the nutrition side. I spent four years figuring out why every diet I tried worked for six weeks and then collapsed — clean eating, IIFYM, keto, intermittent fasting, MyFitnessPal until I hated my phone. Same pattern every time: tight for a month, fine on weekdays, demolished by Saturday night.

Turns out it’s almost never about the food itself. It’s about sleep, stress, your social calendar, and the gap between what people think they eat and what they actually eat. The food is the symptom. The system around it is the problem.

In DND we both work both sides — training, nutrition, recovery, the behavioural side. My entry point was nutrition and adherence, so I tend to catch the macros, sleep audits, and the why-Thursday’s-workout-got-skipped pattern first. Every plan still goes past both of us. If you’ve done clean eating, IIFYM, keto, and counting calories without it sticking, that’s exactly the pattern I want to dig into.

Fig. 03 — Why two

Why two brothers, not one coach.

Every plan we write goes past both of us. Training side, nutrition side. One coach can miss things the other catches — and the cost of missing something on a 90-day program is 90 days.

We don’t bill it as “two for the price of one.” We bill it as “the same second opinion you’d pay another coach for, included by default.”

The disagreements are the point. Whatever survives a Denis-vs-Darius argument tends to be the right call — and that’s what gets prescribed to you.
