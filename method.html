Fig. 00 — The Assessment Program

Assess. Remove. Build.
Track. Adjust.

Every program template you’ve tried skipped the part where someone figures out what’s actually getting in the way of your situation. We don’t skip it. The assessment is the program — and the program is built around five pillars over 90 days.

You’ve possibly been doing the right things — the execution has been wrong. Training hard is the right concept, but doing the right four exercises slightly incorrectly for five years is the difference between progress and a body that hasn’t changed in three.

01
Pillar

Assess

A 60-minute structured call. We map every variable that has to be working for body composition to change — training history, recovery, sleep, nutrition, stress, behavioural patterns. The gap between what you think you eat and what you actually eat. The three exercises you’ve been doing slightly wrong for five years. Most coaches solve one variable. You’re stuck because of the interaction of several.

  • · Training history audit
  • · Sleep + recovery review
  • · Nutrition pattern mapping
  • · Stress + lifestyle blockers
  • · Form + exercise selection
  • · Behavioural adherence patterns
02
Pillar

Remove what doesn’t work

Before we add anything, we strip out what’s been holding back progress. Junk volume in the gym. Sneaky weekend calories. Training programs that don’t match your recovery capacity. The supplements doing nothing. The hacks that aren’t moving the needle.

Less is more, on purpose. More variables = more variability = less control. We start by subtracting. The result is room for the few things that actually matter to start working.

03
Pillar

Build sustainable standards

A program you can stick to for 90 days beats a program that’s “optimal” for six. We prescribe at the dose you can actually take — training, nutrition, recovery, sleep — and we keep adjusting until adherence is high without willpower being the bottleneck.

A flexible macro framework around the foods you already eat. Three to four training sessions you can hit between work and life. Sleep targets you can actually meet. Standards, not heroics.

04
Pillar

Track weekly

Numbers in every week. Training logs, food, sleep, weight, body composition markers. Photos every two weeks. Data is the only honest signal — and the only thing that tells us what to change next.

A 30-minute check-in call reviews the last seven days. Daily access via WhatsApp during working hours keeps the gap between “something feels off” and “we’ve adjusted for it” measured in hours, not weeks.

05
Pillar

Adjust the plan

The plan changes as your body and your life change — inside the same week the change is needed, not after the program ends. A static program is a guess. A program that adjusts to your data is a prescription.

Every plan goes past both of us. Both coaches work both sides — training, nutrition, recovery, behavioural. Whatever survives the disagreement tends to be the right call.

Fig. 05 — Timeline

What 90 days looks like.

  1. Weeks 1–2 · Onboarding

    Assessment call + baseline.

    60-minute video assessment. Initial weigh-in, body fat estimate, photos. Lift baselines logged. Program v1 written within 5 working days and the first session is on the calendar.

  2. Month 1 · Calibration

    First adjustments. First inches.

    Weekly check-ins to see what’s landing and what isn’t. Macros calibrated to actual intake (not what you reported). Sleep + recovery patterns flagged. By week four, the scale is moving and adherence patterns are clear.

  3. Month 2 · Acceleration

    Visible composition shifts.

    By now the program is tuned to you. Body composition is moving. Lifts are climbing. Clothes start fitting differently. The wins reinforce the standards and the standards stop feeling like willpower.

  4. Day 90 · Handoff

    10–15 lbs down. Your own program from here.

    Final measurements + photos. Compliance audit. A self-directed program written specifically around what worked for you — so you can keep going without us. No lock-in. The system is yours.
