Fig. 00 — The method

The diagnosis-first method.

Every program template you've tried failed because it skipped the part where someone figures out what's actually wrong. We don't skip it. The diagnosis is the program.

01
Pillar

Diagnose

A 60-minute call. We map training history, recovery, sleep, nutrition, stress, blockers — every system that has to be working for body composition to change. Most coaches skip this step and prescribe a template. We don't.

  • · Training history audit
  • · Sleep + recovery review
  • · Nutrition pattern mapping
  • · Stress + lifestyle blockers
02
Pillar

Remove what doesn't work

Before adding anything new, we strip out the things that have been holding back progress. Junk volume in the gym. Sneaky calories. Training programs that don't match your recovery capacity. The "hacks" that aren't actually moving the needle.

03
Pillar

Build sustainable standards

A program you can stick to for 12 weeks beats a program that's "optimal" for 6 days. We prescribe at the dose you can actually take — training, nutrition, recovery — and we keep adjusting until adherence is high without willpower being the bottleneck.

04
Pillar

Track + adjust weekly

Weekly check-ins. Daily access via WhatsApp. Numbers in, prescription out. The plan changes as your body and your life change — not after the program is over, but inside the same week the change is needed.

Fig. 05 — Timeline

What the 12 weeks looks like.

  1. Weeks 1–2 · Onboarding

    Diagnosis call + baseline measurements.

    60-minute video call. Initial body composition + photos. Program v1 written based on what we find.

  2. Month 1 · Calibration

    First adjustments to training + nutrition.

    Weekly check-ins to see what's landing and what isn't. First wave of adjustments. Adherence patterns start emerging.

  3. Month 2 · Acceleration

    Visible body composition shifts.

    By now the program is tuned. Body composition is moving. Lifts are climbing. The wins reinforce the standards.

  4. Week 12 · Handoff

    Final review + your own program from here.

    Final measurements + photos. A self-directed program written specifically for what worked for you, so you can keep going without us.
