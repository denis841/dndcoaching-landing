Fig. 01 — The problem

You're not lacking discipline.
You're lacking direction.

You've been in the gym for years. You've tried the bro splits, the PPL, the meal plans from Instagram. You're still looking at the same body in the mirror.

The problem isn't that you're not trying hard enough. The problem is that nobody ever stopped to figure out what's actually broken — your sleep, your training stimulus, the gap between what you eat and what you think you eat, the recovery you're not getting.

You don't need another program. You need a diagnosis.

Fig. 02 — The method

Diagnosis-first,
in three steps.

No templates. No "30-day shred." Every program starts with a 60-minute call where we map what's actually broken before we prescribe anything.

  1. Pillar 01

    Diagnose

    A deep diagnostic call — training history, recovery, sleep, nutrition, stress, blockers. We find what's actually getting in your way before we change anything.

  2. Pillar 02

    Prescribe

    A program built around your diagnosis. Training, nutrition, and recovery prescribed at the dose you can actually take — not the one that sounds heroic.

  3. Pillar 03

    Track

    Weekly check-ins, daily access via WhatsApp. We adjust the prescription as your body and life change. You don't ride it out — we course-correct in real time.

Fig. 06 — Frequently asked

Common questions.

Free programs are templates. Our method starts with a 60-minute diagnosis call where we map your current state, history, blockers, and goals before prescribing anything. The plan is built from the diagnosis — not pulled off a shelf.

Training takes 3–4 hours per week across 3–4 sessions. Add 30 minutes for a weekly check-in and a few minutes a day for logging and messaging. Nothing else. The program is built for people with jobs and lives.

If you hit at least 90% of the prescribed training and nutrition targets over 12 weeks and don't see meaningful body composition change, we refund the program. The bar is on you and on us — we both have to do our jobs.

A standard commercial gym is ideal. We can adapt to a home gym with adjustable dumbbells and a bench if needed — but the program is designed for full equipment access.
