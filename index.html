Fig. 01 — The problem

Years of training.
Same body.

You’ve bulked. You’ve cut. You’ve trained four to six days a week with intensity and progressive overload for two, three, five years. The body in the mirror still looks the same as it did eighteen months ago.

It’s not your discipline. It’s not your genetics. It’s not that everyone else is on PEDs. It’s that nobody has ever sat down and figured out what’s actually getting in the way of your situation — your sleep, your recovery, the gap between what you think you eat and what you actually eat, the three exercises you’ve been doing slightly wrong for five years.

Most coaches solve one variable. You’re stuck because you have three running at sixty percent at the same time. Each one looks fine on its own. Together they’re why nothing moves — and why the program that “should work” doesn’t work for you.

You don’t need another program. You’ve possibly been doing the right things — the execution has been wrong.

Fig. 02 — The method

Assess. Remove. Build.
Track. Adjust.

No templates. No 30-day shred. Every program starts with a 60-minute call where we map what’s actually getting in the way — before we prescribe a single set or a macro target. The call is the program. The program is built from the call.

  1. Pillar 01

    Assess

    A 60-minute call covering training history, recovery, sleep, nutrition, stress, blockers. Every variable that has to be working for body composition to change. Most coaches skip this step. We don’t.

  2. Pillar 02

    Remove

    Before we add anything new, we strip out what’s been holding back progress. Junk volume. Sneaky calories. Exercises that don’t match your recovery. The hacks that aren’t moving the needle.

  3. Pillar 03

    Build

    A program you can stick to for twelve weeks beats a program that’s “optimal” for six days. We prescribe at the dose you can actually take — not the one that sounds heroic.

  4. Pillar 04

    Track

    Numbers in every week. Training logs, food, sleep, weight, body comp markers. Data is the only honest signal — and the only thing that tells us what to change next.

  5. Pillar 05

    Adjust

    The plan changes as your body and your life change — inside the same week the change is needed. A static program is a guess. A program that adjusts to your data is a prescription.
