Fig. 01 — The problem

Years of training.
Same body.

You’ve bulked. You’ve cut. You’ve trained four to six days a week with intensity and progressive overload for two, three, five years. The body in the mirror still looks the same as it did eighteen months ago.

It’s not your discipline. It’s not your genetics. It’s not that everyone else is on PEDs. It’s that nobody has ever sat down and figured out what’s actually broken about your situation — your sleep, your recovery, the gap between what you think you eat and what you actually eat, the three exercises you’ve been doing slightly wrong for five years.

Most coaches solve one variable. You’re stuck because you have three at sixty percent each. 0.6 × 0.6 × 0.6 = 22%. That’s the math of why a program that “should work” doesn’t work for you.

You don’t need another program. You need a diagnosis.

Fig. 02 — The method

Diagnose. Remove.
Build. Track.

No templates. No 30-day shred. Every program starts with a 60-minute call where we map what’s actually broken — before we prescribe a single set or a macro target. You wouldn’t expect a doctor to prescribe before examining you. Same logic here.

  1. Pillar 01

    Diagnose

    A 60-minute call covering training history, recovery, sleep, nutrition, stress, blockers. Every variable that has to be working for body composition to change. Most coaches skip this step. We don’t.

  2. Pillar 02

    Remove

    Before we add anything new, we strip out what’s been holding back progress. Junk volume. Sneaky calories. Exercises that don’t match your recovery. The hacks that aren’t moving the needle.

  3. Pillar 03

    Build

    A program you can stick to for twelve weeks beats a program that’s “optimal” for six days. We prescribe at the dose you can actually take — not the one that sounds heroic.

  4. Pillar 04

    Track + adjust

    Weekly check-ins. Daily access via WhatsApp. Numbers in, prescription out. The plan changes as your body and your life change — inside the same week the change is needed.
